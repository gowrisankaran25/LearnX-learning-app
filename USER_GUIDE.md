# LearnX User Guide

## Getting Started

### Registration
1. Visit the LearnX platform
2. Click "Get Started" or "Register"
3. Fill in your name, email, and password
4. Select your role (Student, Teacher, or Admin)
5. Click "Register"

### Login
1. Click "Login" on the homepage
2. Enter your email and password
3. Or use Google Login for quick access
4. Click "Login"

---

## Student Dashboard

### Overview
Your dashboard shows:
- **Learning Stats**: Total courses, lessons completed, quizzes taken
- **Daily Goals**: Personalized learning targets for today
- **Weak Topics**: Areas where you need improvement
- **Recommended Lessons**: AI-suggested content based on your performance
- **Recent Courses**: Courses you're currently enrolled in

### Navigation
Use the sidebar to navigate:
- **Dashboard**: Your main learning hub
- **Courses**: Browse and discover new courses
- **AI Tutor**: Chat with AI for help
- **Learning Path**: Your personalized learning journey
- **Analytics**: Track your progress and performance
- **Leaderboard**: See how you rank against others
- **Profile**: Manage your account settings

---

## Course Explorer

### Browsing Courses
1. Click "Courses" in the sidebar
2. Use filters to narrow down:
   - **Category**: Programming, Database, AI/ML, etc.
   - **Level**: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
   - **Search**: Find specific topics
3. Browse course cards showing:
   - Title and description
   - Instructor name
   - Duration and difficulty
   - Rating and enrollment count

### Enrolling in a Course
1. Click on a course to view details
2. Review the curriculum and what you'll learn
3. Click "Enroll Now"
4. The course appears in your dashboard

---

## Learning Content

### Watching Lessons
1. Navigate to your enrolled course
2. Click on a lesson from the curriculum
3. Watch the video player
4. Use controls to:
   - Play/Pause
   - Adjust playback speed
   - View transcript
   - Access notes
   - Download resources
5. Mark lesson as complete when done

### Taking Quizzes
1. Quizzes appear at the end of lessons or courses
2. Read each question carefully
3. Select your answer (MCQ, True/False)
4. For coding questions, write your solution
5. Submit when complete
6. Review your results with explanations

---

## AI Tutor

### Getting Help
1. Click "AI Tutor" in the sidebar
2. Type your question in natural language
3. Examples:
   - "Explain recursion in simple terms"
   - "Why do we use Redux?"
   - "Help me understand this error"
4. Get instant explanations and examples

### Quick Actions
Use quick action buttons for:
- **Explain Topic**: Get simple explanations
- **Practice Questions**: Generate quiz questions
- **Code Help**: Debug and understand code

## Learning Path

2. View your 5-phase learning cycle:
   - **Learn**: Watch lessons and study
   - **Practice**: Solve problems and exercises
   - **Test**: Take quizzes to assess knowledge
   - **Analyze**: Review performance and identify gaps

- Track your progress

## Gamification

### Earning XP
- Complete lessons: +10 XP
- Pass quizzes: +5-20 XP (based on score)
- Maintain streaks: Daily bonus
- Achieve milestones: Bonus XP

### Levels
- XP determines your level
- Higher levels unlock badges
- Level up by accumulating XP

### Badges
- Earn badges for achievements:
  - First course completion
  - 7-day streak
  - Quiz master
  - Fast learner

### Leaderboard
- Global leaderboard: All students
- Course leaderboard: Per course ranking
- Compete with peers
- Track your position

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't log in?**
- Check your email and password
- Try Google Login
- Reset password if needed

**Video not playing?**
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Use a different browser

**Quiz not submitting?**
- Ensure all questions are answered
- Check your internet connection
- Try again after a few moments

**AI Tutor not responding?**
- AI service may be temporarily unavailable
- Try again later
- Contact support if issue persists

### Getting Help
- Use the AI Tutor for quick questions
- Check the FAQ section
- Contact support via email
- Join community forums
